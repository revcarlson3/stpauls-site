# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People visiting St. Paul's public church website to understand the church, find worship and community information, or decide how to connect. Church administrators use the integrated admin interface to manage the site's content and operations.

## Product Purpose

The site communicates St. Paul's welcome, worship, formation, and community life while giving authorized staff tools to manage the content and connected church workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public website and admin interface are integrated. The public site theme must be selectable from six variants: three framework-inspired visual families, each with a full-width and contained-width layout. The church's existing logos, favicons, and identity are preserved. Theme customization for colors, fonts, and related settings remains an open decision.

## Brand Commitments

The St. Paul's name, logos, favicons, and existing identity assets remain unchanged.

## Product Principles

- Make the church's welcome and next steps easy to understand.
- Keep public and administrative experiences visually connected.
- Preserve recognizable church identity while allowing meaningful presentation choices.
- Keep theme choices accessible and usable across screen sizes.
